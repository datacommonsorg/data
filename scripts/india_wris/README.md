# India Water Resources Information System (WRIS) Datasets

India WRIS is managed by the National Water Informatics Centre (NWIC), a unit of the Ministry of Jal Shakti, providing a nation-wide repository of water resources data.

## Datasets

- [Rainfall Dataset](./India_WRIS_Rainfall/)
- **Ground Water Quality & Surface Water Quality (`IndiaWRISWaterQuality`)**: Migrated to [statvar_imports/india_wris/water_quality/](../../statvar_imports/india_wris/water_quality/).

To learn more about the datasets and WRIS, visit the [WRIS website](https://indiawris.gov.in/wris/#/home).
