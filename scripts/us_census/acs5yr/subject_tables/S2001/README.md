# US census ACS5YR S2001 Subject Table import

This subject table provides data on earnings in the past 12 months for the population 16 years and over with earnings, including full-time, year-round workers, broken down by sex, earnings brackets, and educational attainment.

Years: 2010-2024
Geo : All geographic levels from country to census tracts in the US.

Notes:
1. Median and mean earnings are measured in `InflationAdjustedUSD_CurrentYear`.
2. Percentages are converted to counts where applicable.
